4. Probability of each option

Estimated outcomes for each legal move with the visible cards entered. Percentages are simulations, not guaranteed predictions. Return is measured in units of your original bet.

Enter two cards and a dealer upcard to compare your choices.

For Hit, the simulation takes one card, then uses a simplified basic-strategy continuation. Double means taking exactly one card and doubling the stake. Split plays two hands, with no resplits or doubles after splitting in this estimate. Results assume the dealer has checked for blackjack when applicable. Actual rules and decisions can differ.
